About Asanteman SHS

Our history, identity and mission

A Proud Educational Tradition

Asanteman Senior High School was established in 1954 through the efforts of the Asante Youth Association.

In 1962, the school became a government institution. Over the years, Asanteman has continued to provide secondary education to young people in the Ashanti Region.

The school promotes academic excellence, discipline, leadership, service and appreciation of culture.

Asanteman SHS is a mixed day and boarding school located at Bantama in Kumasi.
